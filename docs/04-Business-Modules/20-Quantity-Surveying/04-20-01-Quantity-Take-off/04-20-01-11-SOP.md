# 11 – Standard Operating Procedure
**Module:** 04-20-01 Quantity Take-off
**Version:** 1.1 (as built, P0)

## 1. Before you measure
1. Register the drawing and upload each revision on **Quantity Take-off › Drawings**, as a **PDF** or as a **CAD drawing (DXF)**.
   - A DXF is read in the browser at upload: unreadable files are refused, and you see its layers, objects and units. If the DXF has no drawing units, choose them in **Drawing units** (usually millimetres).
   - **DWG** files are stored but cannot be measured yet. In AutoCAD use **Save As → DXF** and upload the DXF. Automatic DWG conversion is planned.
2. Check that the approved company ruleset you need exists under **Measurement Rules**.

## 2. Create a session
1. **Sessions › New session.**
2. Pick the drawing and the **exact revision**. The system never swaps in the latest one.
3. Pick the approved ruleset version.
4. Name it "[Trade] – [Drawing No] – Rev [X]" (suggested automatically).
5. Review the summary and create it. It starts as version 1, Draft. Press **Start measuring**.

## 3. Measure
**CAD drawings (DXF)** are measured at true scale: the scale is set automatically from the drawing units (the bar shows *CAD · true scale*), so skip step 1. Everything below works on a DXF too; see *CAD take-off* at the end of this section for the CAD-only modes.

1. **Calibrate every page first** (PDF), in one of two ways:
   - **Scale tool (preferred):** click both ends of a printed dimension line and enter its real length. **PDF Snap** pulls the cursor onto the exact line end.
   - **Drawing scale… (1:N):** pick it from the list. Only correct if the PDF was exported at true paper size; check it by measuring a known dimension.
   The scale is saved for that revision and page, and the bar shows the resulting ≈ 1:N.
2. **Snap and precision.** With **PDF Snap** on, the cursor snaps to line endpoints (green square), intersections (green ×), midpoints (green triangle), or onto a line (green circle). The status bar names the snap and shows X / Y in metres.
   - Hold **Alt** to place a point freely; **S** toggles snap.
   - **Ortho** (button, **O**, or hold **Shift**) keeps a segment horizontal or vertical.
   - Tools toggle: click a tool to pick it up, click it again (or Esc) to put it down. With no tool selected the cross-hair moves freely without snapping, dragging moves the drawing, and the **wheel** zooms around the cursor. The middle mouse button pans in any tool.
   - Scanned PDFs have no vector lines, so there is nothing to snap to ("no vectors"); zoom in and click carefully.
3. Pick a tool (Area, Linear, Segment, Perimeter, Count). Its **New …** panel asks for the name, colour, fill opacity and draw mode (**point to point**; for areas and perimeters also **box mode**: two opposite corners, **Smart pick** and **Same hatch**, below). With an item selected and the same unit, the panel adds to that item; otherwise **Start Takeoff** creates a new item. Then measure:
   - **Enter** finishes, **Backspace** removes the last point, **Esc** cancels.
   - **Zoom to fit:** the fit button in the drawing toolbar, or **double-click the middle mouse button** (as in AutoCAD), fits the drawing to the window and centres it: the drawn content for a DXF (stray objects far outside the drawing extents are left out), the page for a PDF. Drawings open already fitted.
   - **Hide the drawing:** the **PDF** / **DXF** button in the drawing toolbar (or **H**) hides the drawing underneath so only your take-off shapes show, which makes it easy to check what has been measured. Press it again to show the drawing. It only changes the view; snapping still uses the drawing.
   - **Change the draw mode while measuring:** the take-off chip (top right of the drawing) shows the same mode buttons as the panel (e.g. Point to point, Box, Smart pick, Same hatch, and the CAD modes). Click another one to switch; a shape you had started is dropped. No need to reopen the tool.
   - Each measurement is saved to the item as evidence.
   - With no tool selected, **click a shape on the drawing** to select its item. **Esc** unselects it. **Delete** asks for confirmation before deleting it: for an item with several shapes where you clicked one, choose *Delete this shape only* (the quantity updates) or *Delete whole take-off*. Cancel has the focus; deleted items stay in the audit history.
   - **Grips (AutoCAD style):** the selected shape shows a square grip on every corner and a bar grip on the middle of every edge. Click a grip once: it turns red (hot) and follows the mouse without holding the button; click again to place it (Esc or right-click cancels). Pressing and dragging also works. A corner grip moves that point, a middle grip moves the whole edge; it snaps to the PDF and to other take-off shapes (endpoints, intersections, midpoints). On release the length / area is recalculated with the page scale and the item quantity follows (unless it has a custom calculation).
   - While measuring, the cursor also snaps to the corners and midpoints of shapes already drawn.
   - The **Takeoffs** panel on the left lists every item with its quantity; its **eye** shows or hides that item on the drawing (the header eye does all). Hiding only affects the view.
   - **Quantity follows the drawing:** while an item has no hand-built calculation, every measurement added or removed recalculates it (sum of areas / lengths / counts). Quantities are shown to 2 decimals.
   - If an item is selected when you pick a tool, the panel opens as **Add to selected item** (its type is fixed). Choose **New takeoff** to name a new item and pick its **Measurement type**.
   - **Smart pick** (Area / Perimeter draw mode): hover inside a closed area and it is outlined in green with its area; click to add it. The boundary is traced from the PDF lines, including separate lines that meet or cross and gaps of about 2 screen pixels (zoom in to close smaller gaps only). Hatch lines inside the area are ignored. Text and columns inside the area are not deducted; measure deductions separately. Scanned PDFs have no lines, so Smart pick finds nothing there. **Ctrl+click** works like Same hatch.
   - **Same hatch** (Area / Perimeter draw mode): click a hatched area and every area on the page drawn with the same hatch (same angle and line spacing) is added to the item in one go. Use it with *Volume: area × depth* to measure all slabs or screeds of one hatch as m³ at once. A hatch is recognised as at least four evenly spaced parallel lines; check the outlined areas before submitting.
   - **CAD take-off (DXF only).** The New … panel offers CAD modes first. Quantities come from the exact CAD geometry (true arcs, hatch islands deducted), the same values AutoCAD reports, and each measurement records the CAD object handles and how they were selected.
     - **Pick object** (Area, Perimeter, Linear, Segment): hover over a closed polyline, circle or hatch (or a line / polyline / arc for lengths); it is outlined with its quantity; click to add it.
     - **All on layer:** click one object to select every object of the same type on its layer (e.g. all slab outlines, or all wall lines for a total length). A box shows the count and total; **Add** or **Cancel** (Esc).
     - **Trace layer** (Area, Perimeter, Linear, Segment): draw every closed boundary on one layer. Start the tool, then pick any line of that layer on the drawing: hovering a line previews its whole layer with the count and total, and clicking it traces the layer. To trace another layer, just pick a line of that layer; it replaces the one waiting in the confirm box.
       - Closed polylines and circles are taken as drawn, each with its exact AutoCAD area (crossing beams each keep their own area).
       - Loose lines, arcs and open polylines that meet (exploded outlines, walls drawn line by line) are joined into areas; ends within 3 mm meet, lines that close nothing are ignored, and a line crossing an area splits it.
       - **Subtract openings** (on by default): a boundary lying wholly inside an area is an opening and is deducted (e.g. a core wall's inner face, a slab void); boundaries inside an opening count again. Untick it to take every boundary as its own area. A deducted area is saved as one shape with its opening left open.
       - For Linear / Segment it measures every line, polyline and arc on the layer.
     - **Same hatch:** click a hatch to select every hatch with the same pattern name on the same layer (e.g. all beam hatches `ANSI33` on *Cam-PT Beam*). Use it with *Volume: area × depth* for concrete volumes. A hatch that holds several separate areas (e.g. every column in one SOLID hatch) is added as one shape per area, all in the same take-off item; the total is the exact AutoCAD area.
     - **Same block** (Count): click a block (door, column symbol) to count every insert of it.
     - **Layers** (toolbar): show or hide layers. Hidden layers are not drawn, snapped to or selected. **Only** shows one layer.
     - The status bar shows the cursor in drawing coordinates (e.g. mm). Smart pick and point-to-point also work on CAD lines.
   - **Volumes:** in the New Area panel choose *Volume: area × depth* (slabs, drop panels, footings) and enter the depth; in New Linear / Segment choose *Volume: length × width × depth* (beams, band beams, walls) and enter width and depth. The item is in m³ and its quantity = Σ measured areas × depth (rule VOL-AT) or Σ lengths × width × depth (VOL-LWD). Change the dimensions later in Properties (*Apply dimensions*).
4. In the **Properties** panel (right):
   - Choose the **measurement rule** and fill the inputs ("use …" copies a measurement).
   - Add **deductions** for openings and voids according to the rule's policy.
   - Apply only the **factors the ruleset authorises**.
   - **Record calculation.** Nothing is applied silently; the result shows gross, deductions, net, factors and rounding.
5. Classify each item:
   - Work section (required), element, building, level and zone.
   - WBS and budget code.
   - The tender BOQ line it should feed, if one already exists.
6. Set the quantity status:
   - **Firm** for complete information.
   - **Provisional**, with the assumption written down.
   - **Excluded**, with the reason.
   - **Pending clarification** while an RFI is open.

## 4. Check and submit
1. Press **Validate**.
2. Fix every **blocking** issue.
3. Each **warning** needs a fix, or an acknowledgement with a written justification.
4. **Submit for review.** The version becomes read-only.

## 5. Review, publish, transfer (reviewer)
1. Open the session, inspect the items, calculations, evidence and **Changes** against the baseline.
2. **Approve**, or **Request changes** with a comment. You cannot approve a version you submitted.
3. **Publish.** The approved version is frozen in a snapshot with an integrity hash; this is the quantity baseline.
4. **Transfer to BOQ:**
   - Confirm the target tender.
   - Decide whether provisional items go too.
   - Allow overwriting only if a BOQ line was deliberately changed outside take-off.
   - A failed transfer changes nothing and can be retried.
5. **Excel / CSV** exports always come from the published snapshot.

## 6. When drawings change
1. On the session, press **New version**. Give the reason, choose the new revision, and pick the items to carry forward.
2. **Revalidate each carried item:** marked "unverified", it must be remeasured or confirmed as unchanged before submission (warning).
3. Use **Changes vs vN** to see added, removed, increased and decreased items and the variance.

## 7. Rules for everyone
- Never delete items permanently. Removing an item keeps it in the audit history; only an administrator can hard-delete.
- Every change, decision, publication, transfer and export is logged.
