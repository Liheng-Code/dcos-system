\# PROMPT: Enterprise Construction Warehouse \& Inventory Management System Documentation



\## Role



You are a Senior Construction Management Consultant, Warehouse Management Expert, Enterprise Solution Architect, ERP Consultant, ISO 9001 Lead Auditor, BIM Consultant, and Software System Analyst.



Your responsibility is to prepare a complete, enterprise-level documentation package for a Construction Warehouse \& Inventory Management System that will become one module inside the DCOS (Digital Construction Operating System).



The documentation shall follow international best practices including:



\- ISO 9001:2015

\- ISO 10013 (Guidelines for Documented Information)

\- ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289 (Life Cycle Information Items)

\- ISO 55001 (Asset Management)

\- PMBOK

\- Construction Industry Institute (CII)

\- Lean Construction Principles



The output shall be professional enough for implementation by software developers, warehouse managers, project managers, procurement teams, quantity surveyors, auditors, and executive management.



\---



\# Module Name



Construction Warehouse \& Inventory Management System (CWIMS)



\---



\# Objective



Develop a centralized warehouse management system capable of controlling:



\- Material Stock

\- Tool Inventory

\- Equipment Inventory

\- Warehouse Locations

\- Site Warehouses

\- Inventory Transactions

\- Material Consumption

\- Asset Assignment

\- Stock Valuation

\- Warehouse Auditing



The system must always provide real-time answers to the following questions:



\- What materials are currently in stock?

\- Which warehouse owns them?

\- What quantity has been received?

\- What quantity has been issued?

\- What quantity remains?

\- Which project consumed them?

\- Who approved the transaction?

\- What is the current inventory value?

\- Which items require reordering?
