# 12 — SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)
**Doc Code:** DCOS-MOD21-SOP-001 | **Rev:** R0
**Audience:** QS team. Written in plain steps — this is the training document.

---

## Monthly IPC Procedure

**When:** Start on the 23rd of each month. Cut-off is the 25th. Submission per
contract deadline — check the deadline banner on the IPC screen.

**Step 1 — Create the IPC.**
Project → IPC tab → "New IPC". The system loads all BOQ items with previous
cumulative quantities. If blocked, the Contract BOQ is not approved — contact
the Commercial Manager.

**Step 2 — Enter measured quantities.**
Click "Import from progress" first — review system-suggested quantities from
site progress and accept correct lines. Then enter remaining quantities manually,
section by section. Attach measurement sheets to lines where the client usually
queries (concrete, rebar, finishes).

**Step 3 — Handle overruns.**
If a line shows the ⚠ overrun badge, you must write why (e.g. "actual slab
thickness per SI-045"). If the overrun should be paid, raise it with the
Commercial Manager — it may need a Variation Order instead.

**Step 4 — Add VO lines and back-charges.**
"Add VO line" shows only Approved VOs. Claim up to the approved value.
Add agreed back-charges with their reference number. Disputed back-charges:
enter them but leave "agreed" unticked — they will show but not deduct.

**Step 5 — Check the totals bar.**
Retention and advance recovery are calculated by the system. Do NOT ask anyone
to change them manually — if a figure looks wrong, click the ⓘ icon to see the
rule, then report to the Commercial Manager.

**Step 6 — Submit for internal review.**
"Submit" sends to QS Manager, then PM. If returned, fix per the comment and
resubmit — the revision number increases automatically.

**Step 7 — Issue to client.**
After PM endorsement the system generates the submission package PDF. Download,
sign per company procedure, and transmit via Document Control (the package is
already registered there).

**Step 8 — Record certification.**
When the client returns the certified IPC, open Certify → "certify all as
claimed" → adjust the lines the client changed, selecting the reason code for
each. Save once, completely. Accounting is notified automatically.

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- Entering cumulative quantity into the "this period" column.
- Claiming a VO that is still under negotiation.
- Recalling a submitted IPC to "fix one number" — recall is a Critical-logged
  action; talk to your manager first.

**Who to contact:** system issues → Company Admin; rule/figure disputes →
Commercial Manager; client portal issues → Document Controller.
