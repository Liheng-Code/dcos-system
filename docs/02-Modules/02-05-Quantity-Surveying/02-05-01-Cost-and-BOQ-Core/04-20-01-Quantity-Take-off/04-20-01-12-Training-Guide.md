# 12 – Training Guide  
**Module:** 04-20-01 Quantity Take-off

## Target Audience
- Quantity Surveyors
- Estimators
- Senior QS

## Training Outline (Half-day)

### Session 1: Overview & Navigation (30 min)
- Purpose of the module
- Dashboard walkthrough

### Session 2: Creating Sessions & Manual Take-off (60 min)
- Hands-on measurement exercises
- Applying factors and coding

### Session 3: BIM Take-off & Revision Comparison (45 min)
- Importing IFC
- Comparing revisions

### Session 4: Export, Push & Best Practices (30 min)
- Pushing to Cost Estimation
- Common mistakes to avoid

## Practice Exercises
1. Measure a simple concrete footing
2. Apply waste and density factors
3. Compare two revisions of the same drawing
4. Push quantities to a draft Cost Estimate
